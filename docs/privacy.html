Pulse — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

Pulse is a non-verbal sensory communication app. This privacy policy describes how we handle your data.

1. No Accounts, No Data Collection

Pulse does not require an account. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no servers that receive your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location.

2. Local Storage Only

All your connections, pairing keys, and preferences are stored locally on your device using the operating system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device.

3. Bluetooth & Local Network

Pulse uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and local Wi-Fi to connect directly with another Pulse user nearby. These connections are peer-to-peer and end-to-end encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The app does not read your contacts, GPS location, or gallery.

4. Internet Relay (Signaling)

When a direct connection isn't possible, Pulse uses a Cloudflare Worker to relay encrypted packets between two devices. The relay only sees opaque random tokens — never your identity, messages, or connection metadata. The signaling server cannot decrypt your traffic.

5. Cryptography

All communication is end-to-end encrypted using Curve25519 ECDH key exchange and AES-256-GCM. Keys are generated locally and never shared with any server.

6. Microphone, Camera & Sensors

The microphone is used only in active mode sessions (e.g., Whisper, Breath, Candle blow detection). Audio is processed locally in real-time and never recorded or transmitted. The camera flash (torch) is used in Thunder mode. The accelerometer is used in Bell and Balance modes. No sensor data leaves your device.

7. In-App Purchases

Pulse Premium subscription is processed by Apple App Store and Google Play. We do not see or store your payment information. Purchase validation is handled by the platform stores.

8. Crash Reports

Crash reports are opt-in only. If you enable them in Settings, anonymous crash data may be sent to help us fix bugs. This data never contains personal information or session content.

9. Data Deletion

Deleting a connection in the app immediately destroys all associated cryptographic keys and data. There is no cloud backup to restore from.

10. Children's Privacy

Pulse is rated for users 13+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since we collect no data from anyone, this policy applies equally to all ages.

11. Contact
