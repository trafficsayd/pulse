## Full Description (EN)

Pulse is a new kind of communication — without words.

Send a tap, a heartbeat, a whisper, or a bell ring to someone you trust.
Pulse uses sensory signals: touch, vibration, sound, and light — never text.

**How it works:**
Pair with one person via a 6-digit code. Then interact through 15+ sensory
modes: tap to send ripples, hold to sync a heartbeat, blow to light a candle,
shake to ring a bell, draw together in real time.

**Privacy-first:**
• No accounts, no phone numbers, no profiles
• No data collection — zero
• All communication is end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM)
• Works over Bluetooth, local Wi-Fi, or internet relay
• Keys are generated on your device and never leave it

**Free trial:**
7 days with 7 starter modes, 3 connections, 1 Sneak In per day.

**Premium (150 ₽/month):**
All 15+ modes, unlimited Sneak In, 10 connections, priority features.

Pulse is designed for couples, close friends, and anyone tired of text
messaging. Feel connected without saying a word.
